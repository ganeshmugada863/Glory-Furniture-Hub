Wood Care & Purchasing FAQs

Everything you need to know about our solid teak wood quality and warranties

Q: What type of wood is used in Glory Furniture pieces?

We exclusively craft from Grade-A Burma Teak and seasoned CP (Central Province) Teak Wood. Teak is naturally rich in natural oils, making it waterproof, decay-resistant, and immune to warping or splitting.

Q: Do you offer a warranty on the wood?

Yes! All our solid teak cots, dining tables, sofa frames, and mandirs come with a written 10-Year Anti-Termite and Structural Integrity Warranty Certificate upon delivery.

Q: Can you customize the cot or table dimensions for my room?

Absolutely. Every product in our catalog can be customized down to the centimeter. You can also submit an entirely bespoke architectural drawing via our Custom Request Form.

Q: How does delivery and assembly work?

Our specialized white-glove logistics team safely transports each handcrafted piece in multi-layer padded blankets. Our master carpenters assemble, align, and test everything right in your room at no extra charge within Hyderabad.
