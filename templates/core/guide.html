1. Teak Wood Quality & Authenticity

100% Seasoned Grade-A Burma & CP Teak

  • ✓ Zero Particle / MDF: Every piece is carved strictly from 100% solid natural timber.
  • ✓ Kiln-Dried Seasoning: Moisture content controlled between 8%–12% to prevent shrinking or warping across seasons.
  • ✓ Natural Teak Oils: High density natural silica and teak resin that naturally repels termites and wood rot.
📐

2. Size, Dimensions & Room Layout

Custom fit tolerances and ergonomic spacing

  • ✓ Doorway & Corridor Clearance: Ensure doorways and staircases have at least 32 inches width for easy entry.
  • ✓ Walking Pathway: Allow 30–36 inches perimeter clearance around dining tables and bed cots for comfortable movement.
  • ✓ Floor Alignment: Use felt or rubber leveling glides on marble, tile, or wooden flooring.
🛠️

3. Assembly & Installation Process

White-glove assembly handled by our trained crew

  • ✓ Mortise & Tenon Joinery: Precision-fit wooden joints secured with high-grade architectural fasteners.
  • ✓ Zero DIY Stress: Our delivery crew unboxes, places, and fully assembles your item in your chosen room.
  • ✓ Post-Assembly Check: Slat stability, drawer glides, and balance inspected before sign-off.
✨

4. Polish & Long-Term Care Routine

Keep natural wood lustrous for decades

  • ✓ Daily Dusting: Wipe gently with a dry, lint-free microfiber cloth along the wood grain.
  • ✓ Beeswax Conditioning: Apply natural beeswax or teak oil once every 12–18 months to nourish timber depth.
  • ✓ Protection: Always use coasters under hot beverage cups and avoid harsh chemical sprays.
🔨

Your Handcrafted Furniture Journey (5 Stages)

Every solid Burma teak piece follows our transparent 5-stage artisan workshop lifecycle:

1. Confirmed Timber Selection Raw Grade-A Burma teak planks hand-selected and moisture-metered.
2. In Production Artisan Crafting Master mortise-and-tenon joinery, sanding, and rich natural teak finishing.
3. Shipped Secured Transit Multi-layer protective foam packaging and dispatch via logistics transport.
4. Out for Delivery Local Van Leg Our dedicated white-glove team travels to your designated address.
5. Delivered In-Home Assembly Full room placement, unboxing, final assembly, and quality verification.

Delivery Day Inspection Checklist

Follow these 3 quick checks when receiving your furniture at your residence:

1. Surface & Finish Inspect finish for scratches, smooth edges, and rich honey luster.
2. Joint Stability Ensure joints, legs, and frames are rock-solid with zero wobble.
3. Handover Sign-off Confirm delivery with the delivery crew and receive your warranty slip.
